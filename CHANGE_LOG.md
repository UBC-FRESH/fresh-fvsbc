- P2.2 smoke run. The built BC variant was installed into the spike environment and driven through PyFVS's step API on a bare-ground keyword file adapted from the `pn` fixture, with BC's `FD` species code and without the US `VOLEQNUM`. `init_projection`, `grow` for 20 cycles, and `end_projection` all return cleanly, and `f.summary` gives a coherent 200-year trajectory: trees per acre 173 falling to 90 through self-thinning, top height 9 to 142 feet, basal area 1 to 297, crown closure 3 to 223, and total cubic volume 0 to 13,108. **Merchantable cubic volume stays 0 for all 200 years** — root-caused and fixed. `common/ARRAYS.F77` states that `MCFV` holds "merchantable cubic foot volume per tree calculated in `VOLS`", and `base/gradd.f` builds `OMCCUR` from it. BC's `vols.f` never set `MCFV`; the US variants migrated to it (`ie/vols.f` shows `! WK1(I)=VM` replaced by `MCFV(I)=MCF`), and BC was left behind on the old array. Two lines in `canada/bc/vols.f` fix it: `mcuft` is now nonzero in 17 of 21 rows, reaching 12,751 against a total cubic of 13,108, matching the accumulated `SPCMC` exactly. Four hypotheses were disproved on the way, three of them mine: the summary-label theory, the `METHC=4` theory, the geometry alarm (`min.f`'s debug labels are wrong, not its values), and the `SPCMC`-not-accumulating theory (`IPASS=1` on all 2,850 calls). `mbdft` stays 0 by design: "Board feet not computed in this variant". Evidence in `notes/reference/fvsbc-smoke-run.md`.

- P2.2 correction to the entry above. Its claim that `FVS_DM_Sz_Sum_Metric` differs for all fifteen stands was wrong, and so was the 1431/1875 row-content figure. The comparison treated the literal `INF` in that table's `Type` column as a float, so `abs(inf - inf)` produced a NaN and every one of the 375 `INF` rows compared unequal. With the comparison fixed, that table matches 1789 of 1875, and every real difference in it is in the `gt45` column — the >45 cm class. More importantly, all four DM and summary tables now differ in exactly the same three stands, `SkyRanch-Control`, `SkyRanch-1.0m`, and `SkyRanch-2.0m`. `SkyRanch-0.3m` and all eleven `YSM029-*` stands match the oracle exactly in every table. So `YSM-SkyRanch` has one outstanding defect rather than two: the growth divergence in the three densest stands. No code changed; the notes were corrected.
- P2.2 narrowed the residual `YSM-SkyRanch` divergence and found a robustness problem. The divergence tracks the number of tree **records**, not stand density: `YSM029-246` carries 3699 trees per hectare from just 56 records and matches the oracle exactly, while `SkyRanch-1.0m` carries 4067 from 610 records and does not. Every stand with 371 records or fewer matches in every table; the three that differ have 610, 653, and 978. A scan of every compiled source for a literal array dimension between 300 and 900 found only `fire/vbase/fmpocr.f`'s `CRFILL(400)`, `ADCRN(400)`, and `WPROP(400)`, and those index vertical fuel sections rather than trees, so a tree-record capacity limit is ruled out. Separately, **the build behaves differently at `-O0`**: the variant then processes no stand at all, returning 2 from its first `fvs` call and writing neither a summary nor any database table, where at `-O2` it runs all fifteen. That points at undefined behaviour rather than a numerical difference, so the next instrument is a bounds-checked or sanitised build. The build was restored to `-O2` and re-verified at zero differing lines for `StandStructure`, 110 for the `YSM-SkyRanch` summary, and 375 rows for `FVS_DM_Stnd_Sum_Metric`.
- P2.2 attributed the residual `YSM-SkyRanch` divergence to stale reference data. The `.sum.save` references carry `06-28-2022` in their `-999` headers while the source built here is `ef5fa84` of 2026-09-10, and sixty-one commits touched the BC, `base`, `metric`, `vbase`, and `common` directories in between. The change matching the symptom is `66a5e14e` (2026-03-06) "Sdi reporting update", which adds `ATDR016` and `ODR016` — the Reineke diameter by the Zeide method — to `CONTRL.F77` and rewrites `metric/vdbsqlite/dbssumry.f` by 53 lines; that routine writes `FVS_Summary_Metric`, and an SDI difference is the first thing to appear in `SkyRanch-Control` (QMD 3.45 against 3.44 at 2049, SDI 298 against 296 at 2054). SDI reaches the model through density-dependent mortality, which is why only the three densest stands differ and twelve match exactly. Three further negative results narrow the cause: the residual is not floating-point ordering (`-ffp-contract=off -fno-tree-vectorize` is bit-identical to plain `-O2`), it is not a tree-record capacity limit (the only literal dimension near 400 is `fmpocr.f`'s fuel-section arrays), and `-fcheck=all` reports nothing. Constructing a proof would require building the 2022 source, which is not reachable: the BC variant is not in `meson_options.txt` at that revision and `src/api/fvs_step.f90` did not exist. Separately recorded: at `-O0` the variant processes no stand at all, returning 2 from its first `fvs` call and writing neither a summary nor any table, which is a real robustness problem independent of the residual.
