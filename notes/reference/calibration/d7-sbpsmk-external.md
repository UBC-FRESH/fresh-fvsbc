# D7.23 external screen of the mature SBPSmk candidate

Status: **fails the external BA screen**; no claim expansion

Issue: #416
Build: `fresh-fvsbc-pyfvs 0.4.1`, `fresh-fvsbc 0.21.4`
Evidence: `d7-sbpsmk-external-summary.csv`

The Quesnel mature candidate was applied without refitting to SBPSmk stands in Williams Lake and
100 Mile House.

## 1. Sample

- 7 intervals / 7 sites.
- Williams Lake: 4 intervals / 4 sites.
- 100 Mile House: 3 intervals / 3 sites.
- Domain: QMD >= 17, BA >= 20, mortality fraction <= 0.4.

This is a **screen**, not a validation. The sample is too small for a site-grouped claim.

## 2. Result

| Screen | Intervals | Sites | BA | TPH | Sites passing |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Williams Lake | 4 | 4 | **+16.15%** | +4.34% | 1 / 4 |
| 100 Mile House | 3 | 3 | +5.04% | **+15.47%** | 2 / 3 |
| **Pooled** | **7** | **7** | **+11.03%** | +7.97% | 3 / 7 |

Worst site: BA +26.9%, TPH +38.5%.

## 3. Decision

The fixed Quesnel candidate **does not transfer**:

- pooled BA is outside the ±10% band;
- Williams Lake is BA +16.2%;
- 100 Mile House is TPH +15.5%;
- only 3 of 7 sites pass both bands.

The candidate remains **Quesnel-only**. Do not expand the claim to SBPSmk in other TSAs. Do not tune
the Quesnel candidate against these seven intervals.

## 4. Limits

- Seven intervals cannot support a second-TSA validation.
- The external stands are not a random sample of either TSA.
- The screen does not isolate whether the failure is climate, site productivity, or stand history.
- A real external test needs a larger independent sample or a new cohort.

## 5. Claim boundary

This is a negative geographic screen. It is not a calibration, not a certificate, and not a yield,
AAC, legal, or whole-block claim.
