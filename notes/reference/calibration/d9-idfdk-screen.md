# D9.21 IDFdk2/3 screen: remeasurement depth is the limit

Status: **validation-only; no bundle**
Date: 2026-11-07
Data: IDFdk2, IDFdk3, 4 cm PSP
Evidence: `d9-idfdk-screen-summary.csv`

## 1. Remeasurement depth

- IDFdk2: 353 intervals / 124 sites; **1 site with >=5 intervals**; 84 sites with >=3.
- IDFdk3: 298 intervals / 161 sites; **5 sites with >=5 intervals**; 36 sites with >=3.

The ICHmw2 well-sampled criterion (>=5 intervals) cannot be applied to either BEC. A four-site
"well-sampled" domain would not be a calibration.

## 2. All-site IDFdk2 screen

| Candidate | BA | TPH | Sites passing |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| baseline | -3.7% | -10.5% | 53 / 120 |
| BAIMULT 0.75 | -6.2% | -10.3% | 48 / 120 |
| BAIMULT 1.0 | -3.7% | -10.5% | 53 / 120 |
| **BAIMULT 1.25** | **-1.2%** | **-10.7%** | **55 / 120** |

The best candidate still leaves 65 sites failing; the TPH bias is about -10.7%. No bundle.

IDFdk3 was not forced into a grid because only five sites have >=5 intervals; a candidate there
would be a five-site fit, not a calibration.

## 3. Decision

- IDFdk2 and IDFdk3 remain validation-only.
- Do not force the ICHmw2 well-sampled rule onto data that cannot support it.
- The next step is more remeasurements or structural work, not another BEC multiplier screen.

## 4. Claim boundary

This is a negative screen and a data-depth limit. It does not support a yield, AAC, legal, or
whole-block claim.
