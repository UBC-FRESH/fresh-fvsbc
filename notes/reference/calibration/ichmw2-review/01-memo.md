# ICHmw2 Arrow/Cascadia candidate — decision memo

## Claim requested

An **unsigned candidate** for ICHmw2 in Arrow and Cascadia TSA, DBH >= 4 cm, on sites with at least
five remeasurement intervals, using `BAIMULT 0.75` for FD, HW, CW, BG, PW, PL, SE, BL, LW, AT, AC.
No ingrowth, mortality, or height multiplier.

## Evidence

- 99 well-sampled sites / 569 intervals.
- BA -0.23%, TPH -3.44%, QMD +2.33%, matched top height -0.61%, PSP volume -7.54%.
- 91/99 sites pass BA +/-10% and TPH +/-15%.
- Temporal >=2000: BA +2.0%, TPH -1.95%, volume -2.8%.
- Arrow-only selection chooses 0.75 (63/66 sites); frozen and tested on Cascadia gives 29/33 sites,
  BA +1.1%, TPH -3.1%.

## Decision requested

Approve / approve with conditions / reject the candidate for well-sampled Arrow/Cascadia stands.
The bundle is not a certificate and does not cover Okanagan, Kootenay Lake, or sites below five
intervals.

## Main residual risks

- No third TSA; Arrow is the selection set and Cascadia is the spatial holdout.
- The bundle cannot enforce the five-interval evidence limit.
- The 2005 temporal test has only 12 intervals.
- Only PSP volume is used; non-PSP volume standards differ.
