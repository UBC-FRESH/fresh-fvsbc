# ICHmw2 candidate review dossier

Reviewer entry point. The bundle is **unsigned**. The review question is:

> Is `BAIMULT 0.75` acceptable as an unsigned candidate for ICHmw2 in Arrow and Cascadia TSA, for
> DBH >= 4 cm stands with at least five remeasurement intervals, given the BA/TPH/QMD/height/volume
> results and the stated exclusions? Is it acceptable for operational use as a candidate, or only
> for further testing?

## Files

- `01-memo.md` — two-page decision memo.
- `02-data-and-selection.md` — data provenance, counts, exclusions, selection audit, spatial holdout.
- `03-results-and-failures.md` — performance tables, temporal checks, failing sites, uncertainty.
- `04-reproduce.sh` — one-command replay of the candidate probe.
- `05-signoff.md` — review checklist and sign-off form.
